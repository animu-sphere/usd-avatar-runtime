# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be a welcoming place to work on OpenUSD, avatar evaluation
and the surrounding tooling. Everyone who participates in the repository, its
issues, pull requests or related project spaces is expected to help keep it
that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific, especially when correcting an idea.
- Assume good intent while still discussing the technical impact of a change.
- Welcome questions from people at different levels of familiarity.
- Focus criticism on the work, not on a person.
- Respect the licenses and privacy of asset creators and other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption and sharing someone else's private
information are not welcome. Deliberately ignoring the project's license and
security boundaries is also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

For a public disagreement, keep the discussion focused on the technical work.
For a private or sensitive concern, contact a maintainer through a private
contact method they have published. If none is available, open a minimal issue
asking for a private community-conduct contact. Do not include names, personal
information or incident details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities
should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and privately as practical, and will
give people a fair opportunity to explain what happened. They may edit or remove
inappropriate content, warn participants, or restrict participation temporarily
or permanently according to the severity and recurrence of the behavior.
